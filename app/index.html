Fallaway — a solo card game for one standard deck

One player, one standard 52-card deck, about 15 minutes. You are an explorer crossing a land bigger than you can see. Walk a sliding 3×3 map of cards while the land behind you falls away, dig regions for supplies while hostile cards fight back, claim 4 key cards of one suit in a row, then reach the finish card.

Every card in your hand is food, a weapon and a key: each step eats a card, a high card beats hostile cards of its suit, and its rank opens a key card. Spending a card for one job means giving up the other two.

Play on screen (with a win record and a daily deal), or play your real deck with step-by-step guidance. No difficulty levels: skill wins more often, but the deck decides each game. A careful player wins about 54 games in 100.
